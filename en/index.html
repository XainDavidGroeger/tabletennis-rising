The Story

From basement kid to club champion. And that's just the start.

A story RPG about German club life: the smell of the sports hall, bratwurst on match day, pensioners with long pimples and overambitious parents. Dave explores village, park and hall, takes on challenges, trains with coach Kowalski and plays matches with real table tennis timing.

It's about losing, friendship and a big dream. Grown-up Dave tells each chapter as a memory, right before the most important match of his life.

  • Story in chapters: Dave goes from ten-year-old to pro
  • Timing like a real table: topspin, push, block, flick
  • Special shots, doubles, league season and minigames
  • English & German · controller, keyboard and touch
  • Village, club and league to explore, with quests and challenges

More chapters to come, all the way up to the World Championships.

The Game

Hit too early or too late and you'll notice straight away. Same as in training.

⚡Special shots

Charge your power during the rally and unleash the Comet Topspin. Every chapter adds new shots. Your opponents have some too.

  • Comet Topspin
  • Ice Push
  • The Wall
  • Thunder Shot

◎Real timing

Topspin, push, block and flick like at a real table. Read the spin, move your feet, hit at the right moment: “Perfect!” only comes with clean timing.

  • Forehand & backhand
  • Reading spin
  • Rubbers & gear

★Training & minigames

Multiball with Kowalski, spin training with the Ball-O-Mat robot, knocking cans off with your serve, a stair race up the water tower. Bronze, silver, gold. Gold gives bonus stats.

  • Multiball
  • Ball-O-Mat
  • Can serve
  • Water tower

🏓League, doubles, club

Match days in the calendar, away trips in the club van, doubles with teammates and ranking points like in a real club. In between: explore the village, park, school and clubhouse.

  • League season
  • Doubles
  • Tournaments
  • Open village world

Characters

All characters and clubs are made up. If you've ever played in a club, you'll still recognise every one of them.

DaveHero

Optimist with a big mouth. Cares more about the game than about winning and always wants to smash right away.

KarinaBest friend

Lives next door, actually plays football. Cheeky, honest and the story's compass.

DadFamily

Owns the old table in the basement and lets Dave win, until Dave notices. Makes embarrassing fan signs.

FAQ

Which platforms?

Steam (Windows) first. Android and iOS are planned for release. Before that, a closed Android test with chapter 1.

When is it out, and what will it cost?

That's not decided yet. Wishlist it on Steam and you'll hear as soon as there's news.

Do I need to know table tennis?

No. There are difficulty levels and a tutorial in the basement with Dad. If you play in a club yourself, you'll recognise every shot. And every pimples-out pensioner.

Languages and controls?

English and German. Playable with controller, keyboard and touch on mobile.

Do you use AI?

Yes, and I'd rather say it up front: Solo project: story, game design and code by David. Art, music and voices are made with AI tools.

I'm a single developer and club player. Story, dialogue, game mechanics, balancing and programming are my own work. Characters, backgrounds, cutscenes, music and voices are generated with AI tools, then selected, touched up and built into the game by me. Images with visible errors get thrown out.

On Steam, the AI disclosure is also shown on the store page.

Is it suitable for kids?

The story is warm and funny, with no violence. Bullying is a theme: Dave gets picked on as a beginner and stands up for himself. An official age rating will follow with the release.

I'm press or a streamer.

Facts, logo and images are in the press kit. Demo keys will follow.
