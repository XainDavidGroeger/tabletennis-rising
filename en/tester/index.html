Testers wanted: Android & iPhone

Play the table tennis anime RPG on your phone now. Free, on request, before it's on sale anywhere.

What's it about?

Rising Spin is a story RPG about club table tennis. Dave starts in the basement against his dad, then come the club, the league, rivals and special shots. You play with real timing: topspin, push, block and flick.

What's being tested?

  • Chapters 1 to 4, about 10 hours of play in total
  • Touch controls on your phone: walking, shots, menus
  • Whether everything runs: stutters, crashes, texts that don't fit

You don't have to finish everything. Half an hour and a short note already help. The game is in German and English.

What do you need?

  • Android: an Android phone or tablet and a Google account (the one you use in the Play Store)
  • iPhone/iPad: an Apple ID and the free TestFlight app from the App Store
  • Age: 16+ with your own account. Younger players only with their parents' consent.

How to join

  1. Send a request. Via Discord or email. Tell me whether you have Android or iPhone and which email address you use there (Google account or Apple ID). Ask on Discord soon Ask by email
  2. Accept the invite. You'll get an invite by email. Android: open the link, tap “Become a tester”, install from the Play Store. iPhone: open the invite in TestFlight and install.
  3. Give feedback. On Discord in the tester channel. What's good, what's annoying, where it gets stuck. Screenshots are great. Go to Discord soon

Good to know

  • Android: please keep the app installed for at least 14 days. Google only releases the game once enough people have stayed that long.
  • This page collects no data. I only use your email address to add you as a tester at Google or Apple. Their rules and Discord's apply there.
  • You can stop any time: delete the app and drop me a line.
  • Solo project: story, game design and code by David. Art, music and voices are made with AI tools.
