Android testers wanted

Play the table tennis anime RPG early on your phone. Free, before it's on sale anywhere.

What is it?

“Rising Spin” is a story RPG about club table tennis. Dave starts out in the basement against his dad, then come the club, the league, rivals and special shots. You play with real timing: topspin, push, block and flick.

What's being tested?

  • Chapters 1 to 4, about 10 hours of play in total
  • Touch controls on your phone: moving, shots, menus
  • Whether everything runs: stutters, crashes, text that doesn't fit

You don't have to finish it. Half an hour and a short note already help.

What do you need?

  • an Android phone or tablet
  • a Google account (the one you use in the Play Store)
  • Age: 16 and up with your own Google account (13 in many countries outside Germany). Younger players only with a parent's OK and a parent-managed account (Family Link).

How to join

  1. Join the Google Group. That's how Google unlocks the test for your account. Go to the Google Group soon
  2. Activate the test in the Play Store. Open the link with the same Google account, tap “Become a tester” and install the game. Become a tester soon
  3. Send feedback. On Discord in #android-tester. What's good, what's annoying, where it gets stuck. Screenshots are great. Go to Discord soon

Good to know

  • Important: please keep the app installed for at least 14 days. Google only releases the game once enough people have stayed that long.
  • This page collects no data. You sign up directly with Google (group and Play Store) for the test and with Discord for feedback. Their rules apply there.
  • You can stop any time: leave the group and delete the app.
  • Solo project: story, game design and code by David. Art, music and voices are made with AI tools.
